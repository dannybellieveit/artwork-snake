Media Session config test

How to test each config:

  1. Tap "Play" below.
  2. Lock the phone (or swipe to Control Center / Now Playing widget).
  3. Check: does it show the track title and artwork? Are the skip buttons ⏮ ⏭ track-skip icons or ±10/30s circular arrows?
  4. Try tapping the lock-screen skip buttons — do they actually change track?
  5. Come back, tap a different config link below, and repeat.
